## Overview

We're helping [OrthoFoodie](https://orthofoodie.app/) build ML models that detect foods and predict calories, nutrients, and other nutritional information from single images.

Our current model achieves **36% PMAE on nutrition5k** — a strong result for single image monocular estimation. On warm GPUs with just an A100-40GB, inference takes approximately **0.5 seconds**.
